# Pang Yan Hern — Project Portfolio Page

## Overview

TutorLedger is a Java command-line application for independent tutors teaching primary-school,
secondary-school and junior-college students. It keeps student profiles, lessons, homework, scores and
payment records together in a local data file.

My work focuses on the student book, reducing repeated entry and protecting tutoring history and payment
records. I also restructured the shared Developer Guide and prepared the v2.0 release.

### Enhancements and fixes

* **Student book and school levels** ([#60](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2113-W12-2/tp/pull/60),
  [#84](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2113-W12-2/tp/pull/84)): implemented adding, finding, viewing,
  editing and deleting profiles, linked to lesson and homework history by permanent IDs. Extended level
  validation to Pri 1–6, Sec 1–5 and JC 1–2, preserving identity and records when levels change.
* **Default hourly rates and lesson duration**
  ([#81](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2113-W12-2/tp/pull/81)): added per-student rates, duration-aware
  scheduling, explicit fee overrides and decimal rounding. Existing charges stay fixed after rate edits.
  Integrated recurring lessons and cancellation while preserving older saved-file layouts.
* **Deletion protection and archiving** ([#85](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2113-W12-2/tp/pull/85),
  [#87](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2113-W12-2/tp/pull/87)): added linked-history warnings and explicit
  deletion confirmation, plus archive/restore and active-list filtering. Archiving keeps history and
  balances accessible, persists across restarts and prevents new scheduling until restoration.
* **Covered topics** ([#88](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2113-W12-2/tp/pull/88)): implemented the original
  read-only `topics` command, listing attended lesson notes by student and historical subject. Reusing
  lessons avoids duplicate storage. The later keyword-search extension was implemented by a teammate.
* **Payment correctness** ([#93](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2113-W12-2/tp/pull/93)): fixed attendance
  recording for free lessons, fee edits that changed amounts received, and missing refund reminders for
  partially paid cancellations. Fees below the amount received are rejected without applying other edits.
* **Confirmed command replay** ([#118](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2113-W12-2/tp/pull/118),
  awaiting merge): implemented session-local `history` and `repeat NUMBER` to re-run queries after updating
  records. Selection displays the command first; confirmation reuses normal validation and saving.
  Cancellation has no effect, and replay never bypasses deletion confirmation or current-state checks.
