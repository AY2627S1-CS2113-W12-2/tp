# TutorLedger

TutorLedger is a desktop app for independent tutors who teach primary-school, secondary-school and
junior-college students. It keeps your students, lessons, homework, scores and payments in one place,
and it is built for typing: most tasks take a single command, so logging a lesson is faster than
filling in a form.

With TutorLedger, you can:

* **Keep a student book** with each student's level, contact number, subjects and hourly rate, and flag
  students who need extra attention.
* **Schedule and log lessons**, including weekly lessons, then reschedule or cancel them and record the
  topics covered.
* **Track homework**, from assigning it to marking it as submitted.
* **See who owes you money**, with each lesson's fee worked out from the student's hourly rate.
* **Record test scores** and follow each student's progress over time.
* **Keep follow-up reminders**, such as bringing a worksheet to the next lesson, with optional due dates.

Your data is saved automatically to a plain text file after every change. TutorLedger works offline and
does not need an account.
